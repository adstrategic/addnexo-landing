# Surface Brief: Wholesale Produce Conversion Landing Page (`app/[locale]/produce/page.tsx`)

## Scope & Mode

- **Mode:** Persuade
- **Target Route:** `app/[locale]/produce/page.tsx` (`/en/produce`, `/es/produce`) with URL query parameter variants `?v=cost`, `?v=excel`, `?v=erp` (default `erp`).
- **Audience & Job:** US wholesale fresh produce distributors ($5M–$100M/yr revenue) arriving from high-intent Google Search Ads, often on mobile at the warehouse dock or office.
- **Primary Action:** Book a 15-minute consultative diagnostic call (`Book a free diagnostic call` / `Book my free diagnostic call`). Single conversion goal across the entire surface.
